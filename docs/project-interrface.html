INTERRFACE

TSO-DSO-Consumer INTERFACE aRchitecture to provide innovative grid services for an efficient power system

← Back to research projects

Project objectives

INTERRFACE aimed to coordinate transmission operators, distribution operators and consumers in the use and procurement of grid services. Its interoperable pan-European IEGSA architecture connects participants and market platforms, supporting distributed-resource integration and active consumer participation.

The project addressed demand flexibility, congestion management and balancing, using digital tools including blockchain and big-data management.

Demonstration areas

Congestion management & balancing

Three demonstrators: DSO and consumers alliance, Intelligent Distribution Nodes (IDN), and Single Flexibility Platform.

Peer-to-peer trading

Two demonstrators: Asset-enabled local markets and Blockchain-based TSO-DSO flexibility.

Pan-European clearing market

Two demonstrators: DERs into Wholesale and Spatial Aggregation of Local Flexibility.
