Horizon 2020 · Completed project

FARCROSS

FAcilitating Regional CROSS-border Electricity Transmission through Innovation

← Back to research projects

Project objectives

FARCROSS aimed to increase cross-border transmission capacity and improve regional cooperation through hardware and software solutions for transmission networks. Its objectives included making better use of existing infrastructure, integrating renewable energy and improving power-system observability.

Demonstration areas

MPFC · Power-flow control

Modular control solutions to increase inter-system transfer capacity and reduce renewable-generation curtailment.

DLR-H · Dynamic line rating

Combining dynamic transmission-capacity assessment with monitoring of line condition and analysis of operational risks.

WAMPAC · Measurement, protection & control

Real-time wide-area measurements, identification of inter-area oscillations, and communication architectures for grid protection and control.

EUROPAN · Forecasting & planning

Forecasting renewable generation and demand response using meteorological and geospatial information for regional coordination.

OPTIM-CAP · Capacity allocation

Co-optimising cross-border capacity use for energy and reserves.
